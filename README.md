# Saudi Aramco Business Process Management

### Supply Chain Process Analysis & Digital Transformation

**Academic Team Project | Prince Sultan University**

## Project Overview

An academic business process management case study examining Saudi Aramco's supply chain operations and exploring opportunities for process optimization and digital transformation.

The project focuses on analyzing business processes, identifying potential operational challenges, and proposing technology-driven improvements to enhance efficiency, visibility, and coordination.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze supply chain business processes.
- Understand the relationship between business activities and operational performance.
- Identify opportunities for process improvement.
- Examine how digital technologies can support supply chain operations.
- Propose improved workflows to enhance operational efficiency.

## Business Process Analysis

### 1. Process Architecture

- Examined the structure of supply chain business processes.
- Identified key operational activities and their relationships.
- Explored how different processes contribute to organizational objectives.

### 2. As-Is Process Analysis

- Analyzed existing supply chain workflows based on the academic case study.
- Examined potential process inefficiencies and operational challenges.
- Identified opportunities for improved coordination and information flow.

### 3. To-Be Process Design

- Proposed improved supply chain workflows.
- Explored opportunities for process automation.
- Considered technology-driven solutions to improve operational visibility and efficiency.

## Proposed Digital Transformation Solutions

### RFID Technology
- Improve inventory tracking and identification.
- Support greater visibility across supply chain activities.
- Reduce reliance on manual tracking processes.

### Real-Time Tracking
- Improve visibility of materials and shipments.
- Support timely operational decision-making.
- Enhance coordination across supply chain processes.

### KPI Dashboards
- Monitor relevant supply chain performance indicators.
- Support data-driven performance evaluation.
- Improve visibility into operational performance.
